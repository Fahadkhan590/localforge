Project overview

GreenEdge Landscaping is a fictional outdoor services brand created to demonstrate how LocalForge designs for landscaping and lawn care businesses.

The site balances a premium, nature-inspired aesthetic with practical conversion elements — estimate requests, clear service descriptions, and a simple four-step process homeowners can understand immediately.

Design focus

  • Warm, organic palette suited to outdoor services
  • Serif display type for a refined, craft-forward feel
  • Service grid covering lawn, design, cleanup, and irrigation
  • Process section that reduces estimate anxiety
  • Visual gallery blocks for portfolio-style storytelling
  • Prominent free-estimate CTA throughout
