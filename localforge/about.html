About

Small, focused, built around your business.

LocalForge is an independent remote web design studio helping US local businesses establish a professional online presence.

We work with service business owners across the United States — plumbers, roofers, landscapers, HVAC companies, electricians, painters, and similar trades who need a website that represents their work well and makes contact simple.

Being independent is the advantage: direct communication, focused attention on your project, and a streamlined process without unnecessary agency layers.

LocalForge does not present itself as a large agency with offices, awards boards, or a big team. You work with the person designing your site — with clear scope and transparent pricing before work begins.
