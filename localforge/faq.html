Local business website design FAQ

Straightforward answers for US local service business owners who want a professional website without the guesswork. Pricing ranges and services match what is shown on the LocalForge homepage.

LocalForge builds professional websites for local service businesses: landing pages, 5-page business websites, website projects paired with Google Business Profile setup or optimization, and optional hosting and maintenance after launch.
LocalForge focuses on US local service businesses — including plumbers, roofers, landscapers, HVAC companies, electricians, painters, pressure washing companies, tree services, contractors, and similar trades that need a clear online presence.
Current published ranges are: Landing Page $300–$500; 5-Page Business Website $700–$1,200; Website + Google Business Optimization $1,000–$1,500; Hosting & Maintenance $30–$100/month. Final pricing depends on scope and is confirmed before work begins. See the pricing section on the homepage for the full breakdown.
Timelines depend on project type, content readiness, and how quickly feedback is returned. Focused landing pages and smaller business sites often move from discovery to launch in a matter of weeks when decisions and materials stay on track.
