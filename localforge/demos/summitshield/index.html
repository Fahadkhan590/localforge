Storm damage

When the weather hits hard, start with the facts

Storm damage can be obvious — or easy to miss from the ground. A proper inspection gives homeowners a clearer picture before they make decisions.

SummitShield is a concept residential roofing brand for Charlotte — this section highlights storm inspection options in plain language.

Common inspection checkpoints

  • Missing, cracked, or creased shingles
  • Hail impact marks and granule loss
  • Damaged flashing, vents, and ridge caps
  • Soft decking and water intrusion signs
  • Gutter and edge damage from wind
