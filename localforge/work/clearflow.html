Project overview

ClearFlow Plumbing is a fictional residential plumbing brand created to show how LocalForge approaches websites for home-service businesses.

The design prioritizes what homeowners need in a hurry: a visible phone number, a simple service request form, and a clear list of what the company handles — emergency plumbing, drain cleaning, water heaters, and everyday residential repairs.

Design focus

  • Strong emergency and phone CTAs above the fold
  • Service cards that explain offerings in plain language
  • Service area chips for local relevance
  • Mobile-first layout for on-the-go homeowners
  • Calm, trustworthy blue palette — not flashy or gimmicky
  • Contact forms that capture the issue quickly
