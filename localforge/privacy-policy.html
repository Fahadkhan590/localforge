Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LocalForge (“LocalForge,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website and when you contact us about web design services.

Who we are

LocalForge is an independent remote web design studio that builds professional websites for local service businesses in the United States. For privacy questions, contact us at believerbk4@gmail.com.

Information you may provide

If you use the contact form or email us directly, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Business type or industry
  • Service you are interested in
  • Message content and any other details you include

We only receive the information you submit. You are not required to submit a form to browse public pages on this website.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting environment or your browser may process technical data when pages are loaded. Depending on how the site is hosted and accessed, this can include items such as browser type, device type, general request logs, pages viewed, and approximate timing of visits.

This website is a static marketing site. We do not operate a customer account system on this site, and we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the public pages.

How we use information

Information you send us may be used to:

  • Respond to project inquiries and questions
  • Understand what services you are interested in
  • Discuss scope, pricing fit, timeline, and next steps
  • Provide proposals or follow-up communication you request
  • Maintain basic business records related to inquiries and projects
  • Improve the clarity and usefulness of this website over time

We do not sell your personal information.

How contact-form submissions are handled

The contact form on this website is designed to help you start an email message to LocalForge. Depending on your device and browser settings, submitting the form may open your email application with a prefilled message addressed to believerbk4@gmail.com. In that setup, the message is sent through your own email provider when you choose to send it.

If you prefer, you may email LocalForge directly at the same address without using the form.

Cookies and analytics

This site may rely on standard browser and hosting behavior. If third-party fonts or similar resources are loaded from external providers (for example, Google Fonts), those providers may receive technical request data according to their own policies.

If analytics, advertising pixels, or additional tracking tools are added in the future, this Privacy Policy should be updated to describe them.

How we share information

We may share information only when needed to operate the business responsibly, such as:

  • With service providers who help us run email, hosting, or related tools
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • To protect rights, safety, and security
  • With your direction or consent

We do not share inquiry details for unrelated third-party marketing.

Data retention

Inquiry emails and related correspondence may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential or active project, maintain business records, or meet legal and accounting needs. You may contact us if you want us to review or delete inquiry information we control, subject to any legitimate retention needs.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we receive. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information sent over the internet or by email.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for business owners and adults interested in professional web design services. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the contact form. If you have already contacted us and want to ask a privacy-related question, request a correction, or request deletion of inquiry information we hold, email believerbk4@gmail.com. We will do our best to respond in a reasonable time.

International note

LocalForge serves US local businesses remotely. If you contact us from another location, your message may be processed in the systems used to receive and manage email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after an update means the revised policy applies to later visits and inquiries.

Contact

For privacy questions about this website or information you have submitted, contact:

LocalForge
Email: believerbk4@gmail.com
